# Development Roadmap

Welcome to the **Create: Mob Grinding** roadmap! 

If you're a developer wanting to contribute to the mod, feel free to pick any open task from the checklists below. To contribute, simply fork the repository, implement your feature or fix, and open a Pull Request.

💡 **Have a great idea?** If you are a player and have suggestions for future additions, please open an Issue on our GitHub page and label it with the `enhancement` tag!

---

## Future Updates
Ideas and planned features for future versions:

- [ ] **Mod Compatibilities:** Integrate Create: Mob Grinding with other popular mods to expand the variety of mobs and resources you can automate.
  - [ ] **Ars Nouveau:** Native support for Drygmys or spawning magical creatures, with specialized sequenced assembly recipes for their specific loot.
- [ ] **Advanced Fan:** A filtered fan to blacklist entity types like Item, Player, Mob.
- [ ] **New Utility Enchants:** For both the Grinder and Weapons (like Capturing for mob eggs, etc...).
- [ ] **Rotational Spawner Texture Revamp:** Redesign and improve the block model and texture of the Rotational Mob Spawner.
- [ ] **Spawner Chunk Bag:** A portable bag dedicated to storing Spawner Chunks.
  - [ ] **Inventory Size:** Internal capacity equal to a standard vanilla chest (27 slots).
  - [ ] **Quick Swap:** Right-clicking the bag on a Rotational Spawner opens its inventory. Clicking a chunk inside instantly swaps it with the Spawner's current chunk (or sets it if empty).
